Privacy & data use

MandirSetu performs a manual platform review. It does not provide government verification, certification, Aadhaar authentication, or certificate validation.

Information we collect

To operate the service, we collect your name, email address, account type, and profile information you choose to provide. You may optionally add a contact phone number; it is not used to sign in or verify your identity. Priests may share experience, education, expertise, languages, availability, locations, and expected honorarium. Temple representatives may share organisation, legal-structure, location, and management information.

Documents are optional

Certificates, letters, and other supporting documents are optional. You can register, complete your profile, and request a review without uploading any document. Do not upload Aadhaar numbers, Aadhaar documents, biometric information, or documents that are not relevant to your request. Files are stored in a private bucket; only the account owner and authorised reviewers can access them. Previews use temporary signed links.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, support recruitment and applications, conduct manual profile and document review, respond to support requests, and protect the service. Only information explicitly marked as public for a verified profile or published opportunity is available to visitors. Phone numbers and private documents are not public profile fields.

Email authentication

Passwordless email authentication is used to access your account. A one-time verification code is sent to your email address when you sign up or sign in. Optional contact phone numbers are not used for authentication or account recovery.

Retention and deletion

Account, review, and audit records are retained only as needed to operate, secure, and administer the service and to address applicable legal obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, contact privacy@mandirsetu.in from your registered email address and include only the information needed to identify your request. Requests are reviewed by a person; some audit or security records may need to be retained where legally required. Do not send one-time codes or identity-document scans by email.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@mandirsetu.in. This notice should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch to confirm compliance with applicable Indian data-protection requirements.
